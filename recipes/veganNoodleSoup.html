Vegan Noodle Soup

Found on Instagram here: Instagram Page with Delicious Recipe

Ingredient List

    For the broth:

  • 4 tsp sesame oil
  • 3 cloves garlic
  • 1 shallot
  • .75C roasted sunflower seeds
  • 2tbsp soy sauce
  • 2tbsp nutritional yeast
  • 2tsp chili oil
  • 1tbsp minced ginger
  • 3tbsp miso paste
  • red pepper flakes (to taste)
  • 4C mushroom or vegetable broth of your choosing
  • Ramen noodles (cooked)
  • .5C firm Tofu, chopped
  • Corn and Mushroom
  1. Prepare your tofu by cutting into cubes, and then bake to your desire
  2. Chop the shallot and garlic and add to a heated skillet with 2tsp sesame oil, cook 3-4 minutes or until softened
  3. Place into blender with the sunflower seeds, soy sauce, nutritional yeast, 2 tsp sesame oil, chili oil, ginger, miso paste, pepper flakes and broth
  4. Blend the mixture for 4-5 minutes or until smooth
  5. Place the mixed broth into a pan and heat to boiling for 4-5 minutes
  6. Place the heated broth into a bowl
  7. Add your cooked ramen noodles, baked tofu
